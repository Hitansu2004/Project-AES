EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This quotation covers the design, development, deployment and
support of a complete digital Customer Portal for Arial Engineering
Services — a web-based platform that works seamlessly on mobile,
tablet and desktop browsers (responsive design, no app-store
installation required).

The portal digitises the entire customer-service lifecycle: from
booking a new AC installation, raising a service ticket, tracking
engineer dispatch, approving quotes, paying online, and rating the
job — while giving your internal teams (Ops Manager, CRM Agents,
Site Engineers, Service Managers and Admin) a structured workflow
to assign, track and resolve every request.

Total Project Value:    ₹ 5,50,000  (Five Lakh Fifty Thousand only)
Timeline:               10 – 12 weeks
Delivery model:         Phased — design → development → testing → live



WHAT YOU ARE GETTING — AT A GLANCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • 20 fully-designed UI screens (responsive: mobile + tablet + desktop)
  • 6 role-based experiences (Customer, Ops Mgr, CRM, Engineer, SM, Admin)
  • 18 backend service modules (APIs powering every screen)
  • Light mode + Dark mode support across the entire app
  • Real-time notifications (WebSocket-based, no page refresh needed)
  • Phone + OTP login for both customers AND staff (no passwords)
  • Automatic SLA tracking & escalation (24×7 background engine)
  • Approval workflows for quotes, parts and budgets
  • Complete audit trail of every action
  • Source code + documentation handover

WHY THIS PORTAL WILL MAKE A REAL DIFFERENCE FOR AES
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  •  Faster response — Ops Manager sees every new request in real-time
     and assigns it within minutes, not hours.

  •  No tickets lost — automatic SLA escalation ensures no customer
     is forgotten. Stuck tickets bubble up automatically.

  •  Customer transparency — customers can see exactly where their
     engineer is, when the part will arrive, and what the cost will be.

  •  Budget control — every spend (parts, scope changes, quotes)
     goes through an approval band. No leakage.

  •  Team accountability — every action is logged. You can see who
     accepted what, when, and what they did next.

  •  Lower call volume — customers can self-serve booking, tracking,
     rating and payment, reducing inbound calls to your office.

  •  Scales with you — adding more CRM agents or engineers is just
     a configuration change, not a re-build.
